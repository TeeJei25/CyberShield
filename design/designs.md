## Set 1 · 2026-11-06 · picked Dark Cyber, built
Brief: CyberShield ZA is an AI-powered scam detection app for South African users to identify fraudulent SMS messages, WhatsApp scams, and suspicious emails. The home screen features a prominent message scanner input and instant risk assessments with clear safety recommendations. It is designed for everyday mobile users looking to protect themselves against local cyber threats.
