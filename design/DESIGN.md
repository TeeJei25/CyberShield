# CyberShield ZA

## Grounding
Subject: South African phishing and message fraud. Audience: everyday South Africans checking a worrying SMS, WhatsApp or email. Core action: paste, consent, analyse, understand, retain a result. Moment: before clicking a link or sending money, one-handed on an iPhone.

## Directions
1. Selected reference: midnight blue, luminous cyan glass shield, compact system typography, dense native rows and mascot-led panels.
2. Security operations: black/green mono terminal and log timeline; loses because too technical for this audience.
3. Public information: white/royal-blue editorial safety guide; loses because it contradicts the chosen dark pictures.
Keep direction 1: all four user-selected pictures are the source of truth, including their four tabs.

## Palette and type
Midnight #020B18 → background; deep glass #061B32 → surface; blue steel #123652 → border; ice #EEF7FF → foreground; mist #96ACC6 → muted; electric cyan #19CEFF → accent. Danger rose and caution amber use semantic danger/warning. Always dark, even when device is light. System sans, slightly condensed tracking for large headlines, 22pt hero, 19pt section, 13–14pt rows, 10–11pt metadata. Forms, readers and alerts retain these materials and voice.

## Detail surfaces
Scan: back + title, editable multiline message, channel, explicit disclosure and consent toggle, analyse CTA, loading, inline retryable error, then risk result and reasons/recommendations. Risk does not claim verification or guaranteed safety. History/detail: real saved records and confirmed delete. Example: authored scenario + signs + steps + scan prefill. Guide: short steps with progress, next/back and knowledge check. Privacy reader: actual data flow and limits.

## Motion
Dial standard. Signature: Shield verdict—when real AI finishes, a cyan shield outline draws in a fixed slot and settles beside the risk verdict, paired with success haptic; risk color stays honest. A completed lesson gets one drawn check + completion line, guarded by persisted completion. Token timings; reduced motion renders final stroke immediately. No ambient animation.
